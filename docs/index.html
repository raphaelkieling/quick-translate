Features
01 · Translate

Sound like a native

Write in your language and get a few natural ways to say it, each with a note on tone and when to use it. Not a word-for-word translation.

02 · Translate back

Understand anything

Paste a word, an idiom or a whole sentence in the language you're learning and get natural ways to say it in yours.

03 · Remember

Straight to Anki

Press ⇧↵ to turn any translation into a flashcard through AnkiConnect, so what you look up today sticks tomorrow.

How it works
Step 01

Double-tap ⌘

The launcher opens on top of whatever you're doing, just like Spotlight.

Step 02

Type and press ↵

Use ⇥ to switch the direction of the translation.

Step 03

Copy and keep going

Pick an answer, press ↵ to copy it, and you're back to work.

Learn the language while you use it.
